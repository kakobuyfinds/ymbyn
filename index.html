How to use a HooBuy spreadsheet

  1. Match the listing. Open CAFANS, locate the product and follow its source listing. Check the item name, seller, color and size before copying the link into your shopping agent.
  2. Compare measurements. Measure the insole of a shoe that fits or lay a similar garment flat. Compare the measurements in centimeters with the seller’s chart for the exact variant.
  3. Review QC photos. If warehouse photos are available, match the variant, label and measurements to your order. For shoes, check both sides, the toe and outsole. For clothing, check seams, fasteners and visible defects.
  4. Estimate the delivered cost. Add domestic shipping, applicable agent or payment fees, international shipping and any import charges. Compare quotes using the same destination and parcel dimensions.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the products and prices come from?

The selections and displayed CNY prices come from public CAFANS pages. The two main buttons open CAFANS, where you can browse the current catalog.

What if the source link has expired?

Search CAFANS by product name or item code. Before using another listing, compare the seller, photos, measurements and available options with your shortlist.

Can I estimate shipping from the item price?

Use the packed weight and dimensions to request a route quote. Some routes charge by volumetric weight, so a bulky parcel can cost more even if the item itself is light.
